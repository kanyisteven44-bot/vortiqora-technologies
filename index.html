Straight answers

Before we
start talking.

What is Vortiqora building?+

Our core product direction is trusted identity for physical assets, starting with electronics. We also design practical business and community technology where the problem is specific and worth solving.

Is Vortiqora ID available today?+

Not yet. It is in development. We are prioritising evidence, workflow research and a useful first release over announcing something before it works.

Who should speak with Vortiqora?+

Electronics dealers, refurbishers, repair shops, marketplaces, insurers, financiers and organisations facing a clear technology problem.

Where are you based?+

Kiambu County, Kenya. We are building from a Kenyan context with long-term relevance across African markets.
