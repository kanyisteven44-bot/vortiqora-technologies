What we receive

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, contact details and enquiry. The website’s automated guide runs in your browser; your chat text is not intentionally sent to Vortiqora unless you choose an email or WhatsApp handoff.

Technical information

Our hosting provider and services used to deliver the site may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and request logs. This helps deliver, secure and maintain the website.

How information is used

  • To answer enquiries and discuss possible work or partnerships.
  • To operate, protect and improve the website.
  • To meet applicable operational or legal responsibilities.

We do not sell personal information.

External services

The site may connect to services operated by other organisations, including WhatsApp, our hosting provider, web-font providers and credited image sources. Their own privacy terms apply when you use those services.

Your choices

You can browse without contacting us. If you want to ask about, correct or delete information you previously sent directly to Vortiqora, email vortiqoratech@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained where reasonably required.

Updates

We may update this notice as the website and company develop. The effective date above shows the latest published version.
